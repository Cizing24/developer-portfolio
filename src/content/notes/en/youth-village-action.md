## The question behind the proposal

The proposal concerns local knowledge and youth participation in Longchang. It considers how coastal knowledge, mountain knowledge and craft practices can be recorded, learned and appropriately shared. The aim is continuing cultural participation with respect for knowledge holders' publication boundaries.
